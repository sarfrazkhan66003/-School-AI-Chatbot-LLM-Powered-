Green Valley Public School is located in New Delhi.
The school was established in 2005.
It offers education from Nursery to Class 12.
The school follows the CBSE curriculum.
Medium of instruction is English.
School timing is from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM.
The campus is fully Wi-Fi enabled.
The school has a well-equipped science laboratory.
There is a computer lab with modern systems.
The school provides transportation facility.
Annual sports day is organized every year.
The school focuses on academic excellence.
Extra-curricular activities include music and dance.
The library has more than 5000 books.
Annual fees for primary classes is 30,000 INR.
Annual fees for secondary classes is 45,000 INR.
Admission process starts in March.
Required documents include Birth Certificate.
Aadhar Card is mandatory for admission.
Parent-teacher meetings are held quarterly.
